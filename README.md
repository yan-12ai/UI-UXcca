# Conclusion

## Overview

For our CCA-3 project, we redesigned the Angel One website to make it simpler and easier to use, especially for people who are new to investing. We also added accessibility features for users who have difficulty reading smaller text or navigating websites.

To understand whether our changes actually helped, we conducted usability testing with five participants between the ages of 20 and 23. Each participant tried the original Angel One website and our redesigned version. We gave them tasks such as finding mutual funds, using the SIP calculator, comparing investments, navigating with a keyboard, and trying the accessibility settings.

After going through their feedback, we found that all five participants had a better experience with our redesign. They mainly liked the cleaner layout, simpler navigation, and accessibility options. However, they also pointed out a few things that we could improve.

## Problems with the Original Angel One Website

One of the main problems participants faced with the original Angel One website was that there was too much information on the screen. Although the website had many useful features, beginners found it difficult to figure out where to start.

Participant A, who had never invested before, struggled with terms like NAV and expense ratio. He was able to find the mutual funds section but felt that the website could guide beginners better.

Participant B had to spend some time finding the SIP calculator. Even after using it, she was confused about how the returns were calculated and wanted a clearer explanation of the results.

Participant C faced issues with smaller text. He tried zooming in through the browser, but that made scrolling more difficult. He also couldn't find an easy way to change the text size or contrast directly from the website.

Participant D tested the website using only the keyboard. She found it tiring to move through so many links and sometimes had difficulty seeing which element was selected.

Participant E felt that comparing investment options required too much effort because the information was spread across different sections.

Overall, the original website worked, but it seemed more suitable for people who already had some knowledge of investing.

## What Worked Well in Our Redesign

### Simpler Layout and Navigation

The biggest improvement participants noticed was the cleaner homepage. We reduced unnecessary information and arranged the main investment options more clearly.

Participants A and E said that the redesigned website felt less overwhelming. They could easily find sections like Stocks, Mutual Funds, IPOs, and Learn without searching through too many options.

Participant A also liked that he could move between the mutual funds and learning sections without getting confused. This was important because he wanted to understand investing before actually exploring investment options.

### Easier SIP Calculator

Participant B was able to find the SIP calculator much faster in our redesign.

She entered ₹2,000 as her monthly investment and selected a duration of five years. She also tried changing the investment amount and found that the calculator updated the results properly.

She liked the simpler layout because it was clear where to enter the values. However, she still wanted the calculator to explain the expected returns
