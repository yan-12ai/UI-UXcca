## Interpretation informed by the transcripts

- **Navigation and beginner support:** P1 found Mutual Funds and Learn easier to locate in the redesign, while still wanting clearer explanations of NAV and expense ratio.
- **SIP calculator:** P2 found the calculator more quickly but wanted the assumed return rate and the split between invested amount and estimated returns explained.
- **Accessibility:** P3 found larger text and High-Contrast Mode more comfortable, but wanted a more visible accessibility control and an explanation of Reading Comfort.
- **Keyboard navigation and forms:** P4 found the redesigned flow simpler but requested persistent form labels, stronger focus indicators, and an account-opening progress indicator.
- **Investment comparison:** P5 found categories and comparisons easier to explore, but wanted clearer explanations of investment risks and Reduced Motion.

These themes are based on the supplied transcripts. They inform the estimated scoring pattern but do not validate the numerical estimates.

## Limitations and recommended reporting

The transcript comments are qualitative evidence; they do not let us recover the exact score given by each person to each parameter. The questionnaire labels are adapted from the 18-parameter rubric in the reference repository and should be checked against the original form screenshot before final submission.

For any formal report, state that the numbers are **retrospectively reconstructed estimates**. If the instructor requires actual response data, the defensible option is to retrieve the original responses or recollect the ratings, rather than treating these estimates as original records.
